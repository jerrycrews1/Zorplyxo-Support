Zorplyxo Support

Zorplyxo is a behavior telemetry app for iPhone designed to help users reduce bad habits by logging slips and surfacing trigger patterns.

Contact

Email: jerry@nerdquad.com

Common Questions

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored locally on your device and synced across your devices using iCloud (CloudKit).
No account is required beyond your Apple ID. No third-party servers are used.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes. Zorplyxo supports multiple habits (for example: swearing, stress eating, doomscrolling). Each slip is logged against a specific habit.

How do I log a slip quickly?

You can log slips in three ways:
	•	From inside the app
	•	From a Home Screen widget
	•	Using Siri Shortcuts (for a specific habit)

How do I export my data?

Open Zorplyxo → Settings → Export → choose:
	•	Weekly summary (plain text)
	•	Full CSV export

What information is captured when I log a slip?

At minimum:
	•	Timestamp
	•	Habit name

Optionally:
	•	Context (home, work, commute, etc.)
	•	State (tired, stressed, hungry, etc.)
	•	Trigger (custom)
	•	Free-text note

Does Zorplyxo share my data with anyone?

No. Your data is private to you.
Zorplyxo does not use analytics SDKs, advertising, or third-party data services.

I found a bug. What should I include?

Please include:
	•	iPhone model
	•	iOS version
	•	App version
	•	What habit you were logging
	•	What you did right before the issue
	•	Screenshots or screen recording if possible
